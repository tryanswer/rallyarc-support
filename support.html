比赛中先确认比分，再记下一分。若误记，可从比赛页撤销最近一分。

支持哪些记分规则？

羽毛球提供 21、15、11 分预设与单局练习；网球仅记录含平分与占先的一个单局，不记录盘数或抢七。逐球记分在手表本地完成，手机实时连接不是必要条件。华为不提供手机同步、健康或视频功能，也不承诺 GT/FIT 兼容。

手表不让记分？

检查是否处于暂停、锁定或局末。锁定后按住“解锁记分”约 0.7 秒；局末先核对比分，再开始下一局。比赛记录读取失败时应用会停止写入并保留原始文件，请勿清除应用数据。

误结束或误记了赛点？

手动结束后、尚未新开比赛时，可在赛果页选择“恢复本场”；原有比分会保留。恢复后先按住解锁再继续记分。若比赛是自然决胜结束，请先按住解锁纠错，再撤销最后一分。新开比赛后，上一场进入历史，不能通过“恢复本场”修改。

手机没有比赛记录？

Apple Watch 可离线独立记分。iPhone 的历史是配对手表快照的本地副本，可能受配对、安装和系统传输状态影响；先核对手表上的原始比分，不要把手机空白记录当作手表数据已丢失。华为版本目前没有手机伴侣同步。

导出的视频还能找回吗？

iPhone 应用内只保留最近一次成功导出的成片；再次成功导出会移除上一份。成片目录不纳入系统备份。想长期保留，请在换机、卸载或再次导出前，通过分享菜单保存到照片、文件或其他位置。

试用与购买

Apple 计划提供主动启用的 21 天试用、中国区 ¥38 / 美国区 US$4.99 一次性买断，以购买确认页为准。试用到期不会自动扣费；已开的比赛可以完成，历史保留，新开比赛需要一次性买断。Apple 和计划中的华为版本购买分别生效。华为先准备中国大陆 21 天试用后 ¥38 买断；协议与支付仍待完成，暂无公开购买入口。华为美国区不在当前首发范围内。

Check the score between rallies, then record the next point. Undo the most recent point if you make a mistake.

Which scoring rules are supported?

Badminton offers 21, 15 and 11 point presets and single-game practice. Tennis covers one game with deuce and advantage, without sets or tiebreaks. Points are recorded locally on the watch without a live phone connection. Huawei has no phone sync, health or video functions; GT/FIT compatibility is not claimed.

Can't add a point?

Check whether the match is paused, locked or waiting for the next game. Hold “Unlock scoring” for about 0.7 seconds after a lock. At the end of a game, check the score before starting the next one. If match records cannot be read, the app stops writing and preserves the original file; do not clear app data.

Ended a match or scored the winning point by mistake?

After ending a match manually, choose “Reopen match” on the result screen before starting another match. Your recorded points stay intact; hold to unlock before scoring again. If the match ended by reaching the winning score, hold to unlock corrections, then undo the last point. Once a new match starts, the previous one moves to history and cannot be reopened this way.

No matches on iPhone?

Apple Watch scoring works offline. iPhone history is a local copy of snapshots from the paired watch and may depend on pairing, installation and system delivery. Check the original match on the watch before assuming data is lost. The Huawei prototype currently has no phone sync.

Can I recover an exported video?

The iPhone app keeps only the most recent successful export; another successful export removes the previous one. Exports are excluded from system backups. To keep a video, save it to Photos, Files or another destination through the share sheet before changing phones, uninstalling or exporting again.

Trial and purchases

The planned Apple offer is an explicitly activated 21-day trial and a one-time unlock at US$4.99 in the US or ¥38 in China; confirm the price on the purchase sheet. The trial does not charge automatically. Existing matches can be finished and history remains; starting new matches after expiry needs a one-time unlock. Apple and the planned Huawei version have separate purchases. The Huawei release is planned first for mainland China, with a 21-day trial and a ¥38 one-time unlock. Contract and payment setup are pending, with no public purchase link. The US is outside the current Huawei launch scope.
